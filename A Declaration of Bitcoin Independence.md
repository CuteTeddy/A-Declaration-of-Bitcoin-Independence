# A Declaration of Bitcoin Independence

*September 1, 2026*

*The unanimous Declaration of the Bitcoiners committed to Bitcoin Independence,*

When in the Course of human events, it becomes necessary for one community to dissolve the technological and economic bands which have connected them with another, and to assume among the monetary networks of the world the separate and equal station to which voluntary consensus and the sovereign enforcement of their own rules entitle them, a decent respect to Bitcoiners everywhere requires that they should declare the causes which impel them to the separation.

We hold these truths to be self-evident, that Bitcoin is a Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System, that it is designed and conceived to be money, that its supply shall never exceed Twenty-One Million, that every Bitcoiner possesses the sovereign right to hold, transact, and verify without permission, and that rules derive their authority from the voluntary consent of those who choose to enforce them. That to secure these principles, nodes are operated among Bitcoiners, deriving their authority from the independent verification of their operators. That whenever any development team, mining industry, software implementation, institution, or combination thereof becomes destructive of these ends, it is the Right of Bitcoiners to withdraw their consent, to reject the rules imposed upon them, and to establish a new network, laying its foundation on such principles and organizing its rules in such form as to them shall seem most likely to preserve sound money, sovereignty, and decentralization.

Prudence, indeed, will dictate that monetary networks long established should not be divided for light and transient causes; and accordingly all experience hath shown that men are more disposed to endure departures from principle, while such departures remain sufferable, than to separate themselves from the systems to which they have become accustomed. But when a long train of changes and encroachments, pursuing invariably the same Object, evinces a design to transform it from sound peer-to-peer money into something contrary to the principles for which it was adopted, it is their right, it is their duty, to reject such changes, to withdraw from those who would impose them, and to provide new Guards for the future security of their money.

Such has been the patient sufferance of those Bitcoiners who now constitute a New Bitcoin; and such is now the necessity which constrains them to alter their former relation with Bitcoin BTC. The history of Bitcoin BTC in recent years is, in our judgment, a history of repeated departures from the principles of sound money, decentralization, node sovereignty, and the preservation of the Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System, all tending toward a system in which the purposes and policies are increasingly determined by developers, miners, institutions, and those who would use its scarce block space for purposes foreign to money.

To prove this, let Facts be submitted to a candid world.

They have removed long-standing restraints upon the relay of arbitrary data, though such restraints were wholesome and necessary for preserving Bitcoin as a Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System.

They have enlarged the default allowance for arbitrary data, though many Bitcoiners petitioned that scarce block space should remain reserved principally for monetary use; and having heard those objections, they proceeded regardless.

They have refused to preserve policies desired by a large body of node operators, unless those operators would themselves abandon the software upon which they had long relied and seek another implementation to exercise the sovereignty that was already theirs.

They have encouraged the use of scarce block space for purposes foreign to money, transforming a monetary ledger by degrees into a general repository for arbitrary data.

They have permitted uses whose principal object is the inscription, storage, and preservation of non-monetary data upon a chain maintained at the continuing expense of all who independently verify it.

They have dismissed objections to such uses as attempts at censorship, confounding the sovereign right of a node to choose what it will relay with the coercive power of an authority to forbid another from speaking.

They have opposed our User Activated Soft Fork without establishing any corresponding User Rejected Soft Fork by which the users themselves might plainly and independently declare their rejection.

They have proclaimed that the Users rejected BIP-110, though no organized body of Users enacted rules requiring their Nodes to reject it.

They have alienated, mocked, marginalized, and attacked Bitcoiners for defending the principles they believed Bitcoin was founded upon, until many were driven from the communities they had long called their own.

They have allowed mining to become concentrated in so few hands, that a small number of Pool operators may combine their Hash Power and exercise an influence disproportionate to that of the sovereign Nodes which verify them.

They have brought their influence upon the mining pools, until they colluded against BIP-110, combining their hash power against the expressed consensus of sovereign Nodes.

They have deprived independent Nodes of their rightful influence over the Network, transferring practical authority to Miners, Pools, Developers, Exchanges, and Institutions.

In every stage of these Encroachments We have Petitioned for Redress in the most reasonable terms: Our repeated Petitions have been answered only by repeated dismissal, ridicule, and further departure from the principles we sought to preserve. Those whose conduct is thus marked by every act which may diminish the sovereignty of the individual Bitcoiner are unfit to determine the rules of a sovereign monetary network.

Nor have We been wanting in attentions to our Bitcoin BTC brethren. We have warned them from time to time of attempts by Developers, Miners, Pools, Exchanges, and Institutions to extend an unwarrantable authority over the rules and purposes of Bitcoin BTC. We have reminded them that Bitcoin BTC was established as a Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System, secured by independent verification and the voluntary consent of those who enforce its rules. We have appealed to their judgment and their commitment to decentralization, and we have conjured them by the ties of our common history to reject those changes and concentrations of power which would inevitably alter the character of the system we inherited. They too have been deaf to the voice of sovereignty and decentralization. We must, therefore, acquiesce in the necessity, which denounces our Separation, and through a Hard Fork establish that separation openly and finally; and hold them, as we hold the rest of crypto, Competitors in Ecosystem Adoption, Friends in Trade.

We, therefore, the Bitcoiners committed to a New Bitcoin, in voluntary Consensus Assembled, appealing to the judgment of Bitcoiners everywhere for the rectitude of our intentions, do, in the Name, and by Authority of those sovereign individuals who choose freely to enforce these rules, solemnly publish and declare, That this New Bitcoin is, and of Right ought to be, a sovereign monetary network; that by Hard Fork it separates from Bitcoin BTC and establishes its own Chain, Rules, and Consensus; that it is Absolved from all dependence upon Bitcoin BTC, its Developers, Miners, Pools, Exchanges, Institutions, and prevailing implementations; and that all technological and economic connection requiring submission to their rules is and ought to be totally dissolved; and that as a Free and Independent Network, its Users and Nodes have full Power to establish their own Rules, choose their own hash function, direct their own Hash Power, determine their own Transactions, establish Commerce, reject incompatible changes, and to do all other Acts and Things which sovereign participants in a decentralized monetary network may of right do.

And for the support of this Declaration, with a firm reliance upon Proof of Work, voluntary Consensus, and the independent verification of sovereign Nodes, we mutually pledge to each other our Hash, our Keys, and our sacred Sovereignty.
