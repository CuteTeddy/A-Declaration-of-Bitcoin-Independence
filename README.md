# README

*August 14, 2026*

**A note from the author:**

I put this document together because I want Bitcoiners today, and generations from now, to understand the history of this moment. We did not set out to start this civil war, nor did we set out to hard fork Bitcoin. This separation was forced upon us by the collective of Miners, Pools, Developers, Exchanges, and Institutions of the captured chain.

We did everything in our power to save and reform the legacy Bitcoin BTC. We tried to remain part of the network by organizing protests against the expansion of OP_RETURN limits, running Bitcoin Knots nodes to enforce stricter relay policies, coordinating a UASF, and pushing for BIP110 as a user-driven rejection of the proposed changes, while raising our concerns and defending its founding principles. Yet at every turn, our concerns were rejected, our efforts failed, until the collective colluded against us and captured Bitcoin BTC.

That is why I am putting this draft before Bitcoiners now. This should not be the work of one person. It should be a document that represents all of us. It should be debated, criticized, challenged, corrected, and improved by the Bitcoiners who believe in what it is trying to preserve.

**Between now and "Independence Day" September 1st 2026,**

I am asking for comments, changes, additions, removals, etc. Together we can arrive at a final version that best records why this separation became necessary. I want the world, and the generations of Bitcoiners who come after us, to be able to see the efforts of us the Bitcoiners, Node Runners, Hodlers, and others who fought to preserve what they believed Bitcoin was meant to be.

I see this as a first draft in the same spirit as Thomas Jefferson's early drafts: a document placed before Congress to be examined, debated, revised, and ultimately shaped into a final document we know today as the Declaration of Independence.

The goal is to leave behind a clear record so that future Bitcoiners can understand what happened, what was disputed, what we tried to do to avoid separation, what we fought to preserve, and why we ultimately concluded that a hard fork had become necessary.

Because you can bet that those on the other side will distort history and rewrite their own version of this history, making us out to be the agitators and attackers of the network.

Fellow Bitcoiners, this Declaration is our document and our account of what happened. Let us put it plainly before the world and preserve it for history to examine and judge. Let it record what brought us to this point, what we stood for, what we refused to surrender, and why, on September 1, 2026, we declare our independence.

Yours Truly,
Fellow Bitcoiner
